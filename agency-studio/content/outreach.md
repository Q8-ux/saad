# Founding-agency pilot — outreach and qualification

## 30-day pilot plan (hypotheses)
Target geography: Indonesia. Agency focus: 3–30 client brands, frequent creative requests, public portfolio, identified decision maker.

### Funnel to test
- Research 80 named agencies and verify their own public business contact channels.
- Contact up to 40 with tailored, permission-aware messages.
- Target 15 discovery calls, 8 guided demos, and 5 **paid** pilot agencies. These are stretch targets, NOT forecasts.
- Record real conversion at each stage. If fewer than 5 qualify, iterate the offer rather than pretending pilots were sold.
- No purchased lead lists, personal scraping or unsolicited bulk WhatsApp.

### Prospect scoring (0–10)
+3 active monthly ad/content production for multiple brands
+2 public agency portfolio and clear service packages
+2 identifiable person responsible for delivery
+2 actively pays for tools / outsources part of creative production
+1 willing to consider a paid pilot after a demo
Priority: 7+.

### Discovery questions
1. How many client brands do you actively manage each month?
2. Where do creative briefs and client feedback live today?
3. How many design and copy revisions do you process per brand?
4. What does a typical short-video edit cost your team in time or cash?
5. What quality checks are mandatory for your clients?
6. Would you prioritize software access, managed production or a bundle?
7. Which billing and payment method would your company actually use?

## Pilot proposal after product is ready
- Duration: 30 days.
- Paid price: one selected tier, shown before charge.
- Each brand separated; client materials only under approved licensing/permissions.
- Clearly state credits, revisions, service levels, taxes, refund and cancellation.
- Success criteria defined together: time spent preparing one asset, revision rounds, QA defects and team satisfaction.
- Never promise revenue uplift.
- At end, offer renewal only with explicit consent.

## Agency-level qualification record
Agency / Country / Public business URL / Decision-maker role / Contact channel / Service specialization / Brands served / Current production workflow / Biggest pain / Expected monthly volume / Pilot readiness / Next follow-up.

## Risk and control
- No public upload of lead information into GitHub.
- Handle lead data under privacy notice and restricted-access CRM.
- Use local-language proofreading by a native speaker before spending on paid ads.
- White Label must not misrepresent portfolio ownership or third-party intellectual property.
