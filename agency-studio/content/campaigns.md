# Campaign and content pack — Agency Studio pre-launch

## Messaging guardrails
- Audience: independent and boutique marketing agencies with repeated production workflows.
- Goal: qualify interested agencies for a verified pilot; no payment is collected yet.
- Offer: White Label creative workflow + optional managed production.
- Tone: useful, clear, specific, zero hype.
- Claims: never fabricate testimonials, ROI, time savings, operational readiness or integration availability.
- CTA before lead form exists: "Explore pilot plans" / "Discuss a pilot in a scheduled call" only when a real contact channel is configured.

## Creative angles (A/B test)
1. Fragmented approvals → one workflow.
2. Multi-brand work → separate client spaces.
3. Localization → Bahasa Indonesia and English variants.
4. A small team → optional managed production support.
5. White Label → the agency stays visible to the client.

## LinkedIn / Facebook posts in English

### Post 1 — scattered feedback
**Hook:** Your next campaign shouldn't get lost in a chat thread.
**Body:** When files, approvals and revisions sit in different places, even a strong creative idea becomes hard to deliver. Agency Studio is being designed to organize client briefs, draft ads, review comments and final exports in one workflow — with your agency branding.
**CTA:** Explore the upcoming pilot.
**Visual brief:** Split-screen: scattered chats vs. a clear creative status board. No simulated real customer data.

### Post 2 — multi-client complexity
**Hook:** Five clients. Five brand voices. One workflow.
**Body:** Keep each brand's tone, approved assets and creative history separate. Create localized draft campaigns without losing track of which client approved what.
**CTA:** See how the workflow is designed.
**Visual brief:** Five abstract brand tiles on a clean dashboard.

### Post 3 — white label
**Hook:** Let your clients see your agency — not another tool's logo.
**Body:** Agency Studio's White Label concept is built around your branding in client-facing previews and exports. Your team owns the relationship. The workspace helps coordinate the work.
**CTA:** Compare pilot plans.
**Visual brief:** Before/after deliverable mockup, agency logo as neutral placeholder.

### Post 4 — managed production
**Hook:** Need production backup without adding a permanent seat?
**Body:** Add a managed creative service for fixed deliverables: eight final static ads and two short edits from your supplied assets, with one revision per item. Pilot terms are being tested; availability is not yet open.
**CTA:** Review the proposed package.
**Visual brief:** Delivered-files grid, with neutral sample creative.

### Post 5 — no inflated claims
**Hook:** AI drafts are the beginning. Approval is the finish line.
**Body:** Strong agency work still needs brand checks, factual review and licensed assets. Our planned workflow makes review and final sign-off a visible step, not an afterthought.
**CTA:** Read the proposed workflow.
**Visual brief:** Draft → Review → Approved.
