# Agency Studio — pilot onboarding and approval checklist

## Agency profile
- Agency legal name and trading name
- Country and billing currency
- Business contact email (verified)
- Billing administrator and creative lead
- Preferred interface language
- Written agreement to pilot terms and privacy notice

## Per-brand intake
- Brand name (only in private app database)
- Website or other authorized brand reference
- Logo, color palette, type style
- Approved product photos/videos and license to use them
- Target audience, region, language and tone
- Offers and pricing **verified by the brand**
- Claims requiring substantiation and forbidden phrases
- Approved CTA and destination URL
- Brand review contact and decision rights

## Creative request
- Campaign name and marketing goal
- Advertising placement and aspect ratio
- Deliverable type (static ad / ad copy / short edit)
- Source materials and permission record
- Deadline requested; not guaranteed without acceptance
- Number of variations and included revision count

## Approval sequence
DRAFT → IN REVIEW → CHANGES REQUESTED → APPROVED → EXPORTED

Each transition stores actor, timestamp, version and review note in a restricted backend. Do not expose cross-agency data.

## Required production QA
- Copy factual claims and offer validated
- Proper spelling/localization and brand guidelines
- Asset provenance and reuse rights confirmed
- No copyrighted songs or unlicensed imagery
- Native dimensions and safe text areas
- Delivery format and destination checked
- No automatic public posting without explicit account authorization

## Launch gate (do not charge before these)
- Real account registration/login with per-agency tenant isolation
- Secure file storage, access policies and deletion flow
- Generation jobs, quota accounting and rate limiting
- Human review / versioning for managed output
- Working payment processor and invoices (test mode first)
- Terms, privacy notice and supported market/payment jurisdictions
- Support inbox and delivery capacity
- Verified unsubscribe/withdrawal path for outreach
