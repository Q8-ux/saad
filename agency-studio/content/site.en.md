## Problem / difference
**Your agency should not need a different workflow for every client.**
Brand files in one place. Requests in another. Approvals spread across chats. Revisions lost in long threads.

Agency Studio is designed around a simple workflow:
1. Set up the brand: tone, colors, logo, target audience, languages, claims and mandatory disclaimers.
2. Create the brief: platform, objective, audience, products, offer and reference files.
3. Generate draft copy and graphics from approved assets and templates.
4. Review for accuracy, brand fit, licenses and local-market compliance.
5. Export finalized deliverables and keep a revision trail.

## Features
- **Multi-brand workspaces** — separate guidelines, campaigns and asset libraries.
- **Ad copy drafting** — hooks, headlines, captions and CTA variants by channel.
- **Localized variations** — Indonesian and English first; additional languages after QA.
- **Creative production** — template-led static drafts; edited short videos as a managed service.
- **Approval trail** — structured stages and review comments.
- **White Label presentation** — agency branding on client-facing exports and review screens.
- **Managed creative add-on** — human-reviewed deliverables from your supplied assets.

Not included at launch: ad spend management, direct publishing, performance guarantees or fully autonomous video shoots.

## FAQs
**Can I use my agency branding?**
The intended White Label experience supports agency logos, palette and brand-specific export templates.

**Will the system post directly to Instagram or TikTok?**
No. Direct publishing is not part of the initial pilot scope.

**Does the plan include finished videos?**
Only the managed-production add-on includes two edits from source assets you provide. Fully generated video is not included.

**Who owns the client's brand files?**
The agency or original rights holder retains rights to their supplied materials. Any usage rights for generated deliverables will be documented in product terms.

**Does it guarantee ad performance?**
No. All content needs professional review and campaign performance depends on factors outside the platform.

**How can I join the pilot?**
A verified contact and onboarding channel must be configured before public lead collection is enabled.
