Privacy and data protection policy

Effective 18 October 2026 — version 1.1

Privacy is part of our professional responsibility. This policy explains what we collect, why we use it, how it is processed and the choices available before you share information.

Before sharing information

  • Share only necessary information that you are authorised to disclose.
  • Case documents and details are not published on our public website or GitHub repository.
  • Some tools use external processors; a lawyer must review legal drafts.
  • For access, correction or deletion requests, contact info@sabeq.legal.

Who we are and scope

This policy covers Sabeq Legal Group’s digital contact, booking, assistant and memorandum services. Contact info@sabeq.legal or 22400420 about privacy. External services you choose to open, such as WhatsApp and maps, also have their own privacy policies.

Information we process

Depending on the service: names, contact and verification details; case facts, parties, documents and text you submit; and recordings and transcripts when you activate the microphone. Hosting infrastructure also processes technical connection information needed for operation and security. Site measurement uses a random identifier and records visited sections, referral category, device type, approximate country and active time. Measurement records do not contain your name, conversation text or document contents.

Why we process information

To reply, arrange consultations, verify accounts, understand facts, analyse documents, prepare drafts and research available sources; and to operate and protect the service and address misuse. Measurement helps us understand site usage. We do not sell case data or use its content for advertising. A materially different purpose will be explained and consent requested where required; this policy is not blanket permission for unrelated uses.

Providers and processing outside Kuwait

The current infrastructure uses Cloudflare for hosting and storage, OpenAI for text, document and audio processing in AI tools, and Resend for verification emails when registering. Providers may process data outside Kuwait according to their operating locations, service settings and policies. Content necessary for the requested function is sent; we cannot promise that providers retain no records. Authorised staff may access records needed to serve you. Necessary disclosure may also occur to comply with binding legal requests or protect rights. We do not claim complete data localisation or unverified security certification.

Retention, guests and registered users

When guest mode is available, assistant, memorandum and analysis content is not archived in a user record; providers may still hold connection or processing logs. Registered users’ requests, conversations, analysis and drafts may be saved as service records, and an archive request saves the memorandum. The system requests deletion of temporary files after analysis and sets a fallback expiry; this does not guarantee immediate erasure of all provider records. Measurement records older than 90 days are cleaned up when new events arrive. No uniform automatic deletion period is announced for other service records; retention needs are reviewed against purpose, legal duties and protection of rights. You may request deletion; specific records may need to be retained for legal duties or an ongoing dispute, with the reason explained. Removing an item on screen or closing the page does not delete saved server records.

Protection and security limits

The service uses HTTPS, access controls, request validation and file limits, with service keys kept on the server. Client case data is excluded from public website files. No electronic service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your own document copies, do not share login codes and avoid public devices for sensitive information. For a confirmed data incident, we take containment, assessment and notification steps as required. Report concerns using the contact details below.

Your choices and privacy requests

You may request information about your data, access, correction or deletion, or object or withdraw previous consent, depending on the circumstances and applicable law. Withdrawal may affect the requested service and does not necessarily erase lawful past processing or records we must retain. We verify identity proportionately to avoid disclosing another person’s data and explain the outcome and any restriction. Email a description of your request and an appropriate contact method to info@sabeq.legal. Do not attach a full case file, password or verification code to that email.

Local storage and site measurement

Analytics are optional and off by default. No measurement identifier is created and no visit event is sent until you explicitly accept. Storage needed for appearance, sound and sign-in works independently. Only after consent do we store a random measurement identifier and measurement session data. You can reject or withdraw consent at any time in the analytics settings above the main site content or on this page; the legal assistant, memos and sign-in remain available. Withdrawal stops subsequent collection in open tabs on this origin and removes local measurement identifiers without sending a final event. It does not recall records already received by the server. Choices apply only to this browser and origin and must be set separately on another device or domain. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected even after acceptance. Legacy allow settings do not constitute explicit consent.

Your rights, site rights and service limits

Your documents remain yours or their owners’. Submission permits the processing needed for the requested service without transferring ownership. You are responsible for the accuracy and authority to disclose information, minimising unrelated data and avoiding unlawful or malicious uploads. A parent, guardian or legally authorised person should submit a minor’s request. A request or tool use does not establish representation, acceptance of a case or an agency relationship without a separate agreement. AI outputs can be wrong and require approval by a lawyer before filing or action. They do not guarantee an outcome or suspend a legal deadline. The Group retains rights in its own brand, software and content, respecting third-party rights. This policy does not waive mandatory visitor rights or exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Updates and contact

We publish changes with their dates and explain material changes or request fresh consent where required. Updating this page does not authorise unrelated new uses of existing data without an appropriate basis. Kuwaiti law applies within its scope, together with any other mandatory rules. Contact info@sabeq.legal or 22400420 for privacy requests or complaints. Review an external recipient’s policy before sending sensitive files through their service.
