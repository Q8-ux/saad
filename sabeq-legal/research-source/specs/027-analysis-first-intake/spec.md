# Analysis-first conversation correction
The latest user instruction supersedes the earlier requirement to collect every pleading field in chat. Extract supplied facts from the full conversation, show a concise provisional analysis, and ask at most one genuinely blocking factual question. Do not turn missing names, addresses, case/chamber numbers or document availability into a mandatory interview. Keep unknown details empty and collect the final form fields in MemoWizard. Preparing a draft is distinct from a fully reviewed handoff: preserve all source, procedural, payment and final-generation guards, do not invent facts or authorities, and retain human review.
Acceptance: a clear story/role/request can move to preparation without all administrative details; contradictions, unknown appeal actor or absent facts/relief still prompt once; answered/unavailable questions never repeat; analysis survives the final guard; incomplete form data never skips directly to final review.
